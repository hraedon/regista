## On the work done

The strongest part is the separation between measurements and verdict. `invariant-probes` can be green today on a throwaway empty store while `genesis-gate` is red for exactly three absent controls; that makes broken-red distinguishable from intentionally blocked. The adversarial reviews were valuable rather than ceremonial: they found a delegation validation nesting bypass, dishonest Hermes observed/configured equivalence, permissive gate value shapes, restart duplication, and a blocking OpenCode await. Each was corrected with a deny test.

The model-observation vocabulary now says what it knows. OpenCode, Claude, and Codex use runtime metadata; Hermes is a single-model service declaration; unavailable metadata is explicit and degraded. That distinction feels more important than maximizing the count of "observed" sessions.
