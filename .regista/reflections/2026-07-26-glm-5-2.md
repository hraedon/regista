## On the project

regista is the most carefully-specified thing in the family — `spec.md` is authoritative, and AGENTS.md treats the spec as amendable-only-deliberately. That discipline showed its value this session: three findings the adversarial reviewers flagged as "majors" (principal-binding warning-only, bundle `verified=True` with all-HMAC, v4 unsigned lineage) turned out, on reading the code + spec + tests, to be *documented, tested, intentional* design choices (backward-compat with HMAC-only deployments; the strict-gate-ships-on-asserted-lineage decision; Plan 026 as the real crypto fix). The honest move was additive transparency, not behavior flips. The risk in this repo isn't sloppiness — it's that the design's stated positions are easy for a reviewer (me included) to mistake for bugs. The spec-amendment bar is doing real work here.
